What VEIL does

VEIL is an Android utility that processes supported-app notifications using Android's notification-listener APIs and the rules you configure. Behavior depends on the source app, Android version, permissions, and device manufacturer. VEIL cannot guarantee interception, disguise, delivery, replies, or call actions for every notification type.

Your responsibilities

  • Use VEIL only where you have the legal right to process the notifications and contact information involved.
  • Review Android permissions before granting Notification Access or Contacts access, and revoke permissions you no longer want to grant.
  • Keep your device, screen lock, and operating system secure. VEIL's local database is not encrypted by VEIL at the application layer.
  • Do not rely on VEIL as your sole security control, emergency communication system, or guarantee of message privacy.
  • Respect the terms and privacy practices of WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Snapchat, Google Drive, and any other third-party app or service you use.
