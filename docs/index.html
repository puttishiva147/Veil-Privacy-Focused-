About VEIL

VEIL is designed to give you a focused place to manage selected-contact protection and notification-related events on your Android device. Its features depend on Android permissions and device settings that you choose to enable.

Selected contactsConfigure protection rules for contacts you choose.
Private event viewReview app-managed notification events in a dedicated interface.
Device controlsAccess security settings and supported biometric authentication.
